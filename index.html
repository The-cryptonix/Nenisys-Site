FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Nenisys?

Nenisys is a technology company based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, building products in trading and education. Its products are Mercora Edge, a proprietary trading firm, and Edutech AI, an AI-powered learning app.

Who owns and leads Nenisys?

Nenisys is owned and led by Christian Godliness Chimenem, CEO & Owner. He is a software engineer who is also studying Physics at undergraduate level.

What is Mercora Edge?

Mercora Edge is a proprietary trading firm that evaluates traders and provides funded accounts to qualified traders, built on an in-house trading engine. It was previously developed under the working name Genex.

Who owns Mercora Edge?

Mercora Edge is owned and operated by Nenisys, whose CEO and owner is Christian Godliness Chimenem.

When does Mercora Edge launch?

Mercora Edge launches on 30 October 2026.

What is Edutech AI?

Edutech AI is a learning app in development by Nenisys. Students write or draw on a phone screen or whiteboard, and an AI replies with feedback based on both the text and the drawing.

How can I contact Nenisys?

Email hello@nenisys.shop. Nenisys is based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria.
